The Vision · Why We're Staging This

One membership. One night out. One marquee, everywhere.

House Seats already owns the hardest thing to build: a trusted relationship with an entertainment audience. The House Experience turns that trust into a category — a single brand that decides how a city goes out. Presents is the front door. Seats, Eats and Experience are the rooms behind it.

Word of Mouth

“Ask House.”

Where the answer already lives

The insight

Fill the house first, sell second

The weekly editorial keeps the audience engaged. Presents monetizes that attention. Every later layer — tickets, dining, experiences — rides on an audience that already shows up every week.

The prize

A show you can take on tour

What works in Vegas becomes the touring set: local editorial, local seats, local eats — under one marquee. The Presents opening is where that playbook is written and proven.

The Booking · Act One

5,000 seats sold by opening night — earned, not assumed

Leadership believes ~5,000 of the 14,000+ members could subscribe to a renewed Presents — a ~35.7% conversion, grounded in the prior concept's performance. Step one is to validate that belief with data before a dollar is committed.

Validate before we build

— Prior subscriber counts & conversion rates
— Historical pricing, retention & churn
— Email & content performance
— Acquisition sources that actually worked
— Why the earlier product changed or ended

The 5,000 Presents target and the separate 2027 goal of +5,000 core members are tracked independently.

Working benchmark

Revenue, not profit. Fees, content and operations are modeled later in the deck.
